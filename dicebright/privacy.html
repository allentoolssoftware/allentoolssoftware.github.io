Privacy Policy for Dicebright

Effective date: November 15, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Dicebright ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app is published by Jack Allen (Allen Tools Software), an individual developer.

Summary

  • Dicebright works on your device. There are no accounts, and we (the developer) do not run servers and do not receive your data.
  • Your dice, saved roll sets, roll history and settings stay on your device. We never see them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's ads software collects some device and usage data to show ads, as described below. We request non-personalized ads only and never ask to track you across apps. Once your "Remove ads" purchase is recognized on your device, the ads software is no longer started. (On a new device or after reinstalling, the app first checks your purchase with the App Store, which takes a few seconds.)
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we instruct Google to restrict how it processes ad data (see below).

Information stored on your device

The app saves your dice, saved roll sets, roll history and settings in the app's local storage on your device. It is never sent to us. You can delete your dice, roll sets and history at any time in Settings > Reset everything (your settings are kept), or delete everything by deleting the app. If you use device or iCloud backups, Apple may include this data in your backups under Apple's own privacy policy.

Shake to roll

If "Shake to roll" is on, the app reads your device's motion sensor while the app is open, only to notice a shake. Motion readings are used instantly on your device and are never stored or sent anywhere. You can turn this off in Settings.

Information we receive

We, the developer, do not receive your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, camera or microphone data, or your rolls. The app does not ask for permission to use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

If you email us for support or to report an ad, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect information such as:

  • your device's IP address (which can indicate your approximate region),
  • device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, app version and language),
  • device identifiers that are specific to this app or this developer (not the advertising identifier used for cross-app tracking),
  • ad interaction information (for example, which ads were shown or tapped),
  • diagnostic and performance information about the ads software (for example, load times and crashes).

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The app requests non-personalized ads only, meaning ads are not based on your past behavior, and it sends Google's "restricted data processing" signal, which tells Google to limit its use of ad data under US state privacy laws. The app does not ask for permission to track you and does not give Google access to your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). Where required by law, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choices in Settings > Ad privacy choices when that option is shown.

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Purchases

If you buy "Remove ads", the purchase is processed by Apple (or Google on Android). We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from the store that the purchase was made, and remembers it on your device.

Diagnostics from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous usage and crash statistics. We use them only to fix problems and improve the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your dice, roll sets and history at any time in Settings > Reset everything, or all app data by deleting the app.
  • Remove ads with a one-time purchase.
  • California residents: we do not sell your personal information, and we ask Google to restrict its processing of ad data as described above. You may ask what personal information we hold about you (normally none, or only a support email) and ask us to delete it by contacting us. We will not discriminate against you for making a request.

Do Not Track and other apps

The app does not respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals, because it is not a website and does not track you across other apps or websites. Apart from Google's ads software described above, no third party collects information through the app.

Data security and retention

Data in the app stays on your device and is protected by your device's security. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you.

Availability

The app is offered only in the countries listed on its App Store page. It is not offered in the European Union.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at https://allentoolssoftware.github.io/dicebright/privacy.html. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
