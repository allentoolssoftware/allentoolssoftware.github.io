How to use it

Tap a die type (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 or d%) to add it to the tray, then tap Roll or shake your phone. Tap a die to hold it: held dice keep their value when you roll again. Press and hold a die to remove it. Use + and − to add a modifier to the total. Save any combination as a roll set to bring it back in one tap.

Common questions

  • Are the rolls random? Yes. Every die is rolled with your device's random number generator, so each face has the same chance.
  • Where are my roll sets stored? Only on your device. There is no account or cloud copy, so deleting the app deletes them.
  • Shake to roll isn't working. Check that "Shake to roll" is on in Settings. Shaking only works while the app is open on the roll screen.
  • I bought Remove Ads on a new phone. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase.
