Scorequill Support

Need help, found a bug, or want to report an inappropriate ad? Email allentoolssoftware@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

  • Where are my games stored? Only on your device (and in your own device backups, if you use them). There is no account or cloud copy from us, so deleting the app deletes your games.
  • I bought Remove Ads on a new phone. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase.
  • How do I fix a wrong score? Open the game, tap Round sheet, then tap the cell to correct it. You can also tap Undo.
