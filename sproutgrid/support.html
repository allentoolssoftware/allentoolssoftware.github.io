How to play

Plant exactly one sprout in every row, every column and every garden bed. Sprouts can't touch each other, not even diagonally. Tap a square once to mark it as empty, twice to plant a sprout, and a third time to clear it. In bigger gardens, rocks block some squares: nothing can grow on a rock.

Common questions

  • Where is my progress stored? Only on your device. There is no account or cloud copy, so deleting the app deletes your progress.
  • I bought Remove Ads on a new phone. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase.
  • Every puzzle has one answer? Yes. Each puzzle is checked to have exactly one solution, so it can always be solved by logic alone.
